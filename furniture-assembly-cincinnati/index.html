The assembly process

From first photos to ready to use.

Plain, careful steps keep the scope clear and prevent missing or damaged parts from being buried inside the finished piece.

01

Review the product and location

I look at the product link or model, item count, intended room, access and any lifting or anchoring concerns.

02

Confirm scope and price

You receive a project-based price and clear expectations before the appointment is scheduled.

03

Protect the work area

The floor and nearby surfaces are considered before boxes, panels and tools are laid out.

04

Inventory and organize

Panels, fasteners and hardware are identified and arranged so missing or damaged pieces are easier to catch.

05

Follow the intended sequence

The manufacturer’s instructions guide the build instead of forcing parts together or skipping structural steps.

06

Check alignment and stability

Fasteners, panels, shelves and the overall structure are checked and adjusted where the design allows.

07

Test moving parts

Drawers, doors, casters, brakes and adjustable components are operated when applicable.

08

Discuss anti-tip anchoring

Manufacturer-specified anchoring is discussed and reviewed around the supplied hardware and wall.

09

Document problems honestly

If an essential part is missing or damaged, I identify it instead of hiding the problem or improvising an unsafe repair.

10

Gather and clean up

Packaging is gathered and the immediate work area is left orderly, with off-site disposal handled only when separately agreed.

Not currently offered

Condition matters. Previously damaged or badly misassembled particleboard furniture—and items missing essential instructions, hardware or structural components—may not be safe or practical to complete.

No hidden problems

If the box has a problem, you deserve to know.

Flat-pack furniture can arrive with missing hardware, incorrect panels, shipping damage or holes weakened by an earlier assembly attempt.

Missing or damaged parts

I’ll stop at the appropriate point, show you what I found and document the issue so you can contact the retailer or manufacturer. I won’t conceal the problem inside the piece.

Previously misassembled furniture

Correction work can be reviewed, but damaged fastener holes or particleboard may limit what can be made safe. An honest “this should be replaced” is better than a temporary repair presented as finished.

Frequently asked questions

What homeowners usually want to know.

Furniture varies widely. Product information and room photos make it possible to give you a useful answer before scheduling.

What types of furniture do you assemble?

Selected residential pieces include desks, bookshelves, freestanding storage, dressers, nightstands, tables, standard chairs, TV stands, media consoles, freestanding kitchen carts and standard bed frames after model review. Permanent kitchen islands and built-in cabinetry are not offered.

Do you assemble IKEA, Wayfair and Amazon furniture?

Yes, selected flat-pack furniture from IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon and other retailers can be reviewed by product link, brand or model. The Covedale Craftsman is an independent service and is not affiliated with those retailers.

Can you assemble several pieces during one visit?

Yes. Furniture assembly is best suited for one substantial piece or several items completed during the same visit. Send every product link and the item count so the complete scope can be priced first.

Do you anchor dressers and bookshelves to the wall?

Manufacturer-specified anti-tip anchoring may be available after reviewing the furniture, supplied hardware, wall construction and intended location. Not every furniture-and-wall combination can be accepted.

What happens if parts are missing or damaged?

I’ll identify and document the issue, show you what I found and discuss the next step. I won’t hide the problem or improvise an unsafe repair simply to call the item complete.

Do the boxes need to be in the intended room?

Ideally, unopened boxes should be in or near the intended room. If that is not practical, tell me where they are, whether stairs are involved and whether there are lifting or access concerns.

Will you clean up and take the furniture packaging with you?

I gather the boxes, plastic and packing material and leave the immediate work area clean. Taking the packaging away from your home is not included unless we agree to it before the appointment.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the product link, brand or model, number of items, photos of the boxes or furniture, a photo of the intended location, and any known lifting, access or anchoring concerns.

Do you repair furniture that was assembled incorrectly?

Some correction work can be reviewed. Acceptance depends on the item’s condition and whether panels, fastener holes or structural parts have already been damaged. Badly damaged particleboard may not be safely repairable.

Which Cincinnati neighborhoods do you serve?

I primarily serve Cincinnati’s West Side and nearby communities when the project is a fit. Text your address or ZIP code if you are unsure.
