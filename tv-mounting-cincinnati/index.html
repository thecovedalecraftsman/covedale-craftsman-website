A carefully screened service

Straightforward residential installations—clearly defined.

Every TV, mount and wall is different. Compatibility, wall construction, mounting height and lifting requirements are reviewed before the installation is accepted.

Standard installations may include

  • Select fixed, tilting and full-motion mounts
  • Drywall over confirmed wood studs
  • Placement discussion and approval
  • Homeowner-provided compatible mounts
  • Visible cable organization
  • Connection, testing and walkthrough

Available after review

  • Larger or unusually heavy televisions
  • Plaster, paneling or uncertain wall construction
  • Existing mount removal or replacement
  • Reuse of an identifiable compatible mount
  • Selected soundbar or shelf mounting
  • Minor holes or selected low-voltage routing

Not currently offered

  • Fireplace installations
  • Outdoor or ceiling-mounted TVs
  • Special corner or extremely high installations
  • Tile, stone or difficult masonry
  • Commercial installations
  • Damaged walls or walls without suitable support

A suitable existing receptacle is required. New or relocated outlets are not included in the standard TV-mounting service and can only be considered as separate work.

Cable management without unsafe shortcuts

Organized cables—with clear limits.

“Cable management” should never hide what the installation actually includes.

The right approach depends on the outlet, connected equipment, wall construction and the appearance you want. The available option is agreed upon before the work begins.

Visible cable organization

Cables can be bundled behind the television and routed neatly toward nearby furniture or equipment.

Paintable surface raceway

A surface-mounted cable cover may provide a cleaner finished route without opening the wall.

Selected low-voltage routing

Routing HDMI or other low-voltage cables through an open wall cavity is not automatic and requires evaluation first.

Power stays safe.

Ordinary TV power cords and extension cords will never be run inside a wall. If the planned location lacks suitable power, the position or electrical plan must change.

Frequently asked questions

What homeowners usually want to know.

Exact requirements vary by television, mount, wall construction, height and connected equipment.

What types of TV mounts do you install?

The standard service includes select fixed, tilting and full-motion mounts after compatibility review. Every installation is still screened for TV and mount compatibility, wall construction, mounting height and a safe lifting plan.

Does the homeowner provide the mount?

Preferably, yes. Send the TV and mount models before the appointment so compatibility can be reviewed. Purchasing or pickup can be discussed when needed.

What wall types do you mount TVs on?

Standard service focuses on drywall over suitable wood studs. Plaster, paneling and other wall construction must be reviewed before scheduling.

Is there a maximum TV size?

There is no public size promise. Acceptance depends on the television model, weight, dimensions, mounting height and a safe lifting plan. Larger or unusually heavy TVs are reviewed individually.

Can you hide the cables?

Visible cable organization and paintable surface raceway are available. In-wall low-voltage routing is not part of every installation and requires evaluation. Ordinary TV power cords and extension cords are never concealed inside walls.

Can you connect my soundbar or other devices?

Yes. Familiar soundbars, cable boxes, game systems, disc players, antennas and selected equipment can be connected and tested when included in the agreed scope.

Can you remove or replace an existing mount?

Often, after reviewing the existing mount, wall condition, hardware and desired new location. Reuse requires an identifiable, compatible mount in good condition with the correct hardware.

Do you mount televisions above fireplaces?

No. Fireplace installations are not currently offered.
