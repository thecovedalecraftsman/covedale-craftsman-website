Worth considering first

Questions worth answering before the camera goes up.

Small placement decisions can determine whether the camera is useful every day or frustrating from the beginning.

Is this the right height?

Higher is not always better. The useful height depends on the area and detail you want to capture.

Will it see the intended area?

A small change in angle or position can prevent an avoidable blind spot.

Can the battery be reached?

Battery-powered cameras should remain practical to charge or remove later.

Is the surface suitable?

Brick, concrete, wood, siding and soffit materials require different mounting decisions.

What could block the view?

Walls, overhangs, trim, gutters, lights and landscaping can affect coverage.

What can the camera realistically do?

Consumer cameras are useful tools, but expectations are best discussed before installation.

Frequently asked questions

What homeowners usually want to know.

Exact requirements vary by camera, location, mounting surface and available power.

Can you install cameras I already purchased?

Yes. Most projects involve homeowner-purchased cameras. Send the brand, model and photos of the proposed installation locations so I can confirm whether the project is a fit.

Which residential camera brands do you work with?

I work with common homeowner systems such as Ring, Arlo, Eufy, Blink, Nest and similar equipment. The exact installation depends on the model, mounting hardware, power requirements and location.

Can you mount security cameras on brick or concrete?

Yes, when the location and equipment are suitable. Completed residential work includes Ring cameras mounted on exterior brick and garage concrete.

Do you install battery-powered cameras?

Yes. Placement should consider both the finished camera view and future access for charging or battery removal.

Can you help decide where the cameras should go?

Yes. We discuss what you want each camera to see and review the proposed height, angle, mounting surface and surrounding features before installation.

Can you install several cameras during one visit?

Yes, depending on the number of cameras, mounting locations and overall scope. Send photos of every proposed location so the work can be evaluated accurately.

Do you install floodlight or wired security cameras?

Wired and floodlight-camera installation is not offered unless the specific work has been confirmed compatible with applicable licensing, permit, inspection, and insurance requirements.

Can you help with a camera that is already installed but not working?

Possibly. Straightforward residential troubleshooting may be available when the project is a fit. Problems involving defective equipment, account restrictions, weak Wi-Fi, damaged wiring or issues outside the service scope may require a different next step.

Do you provide advanced app or network configuration?

Most homeowners already have their account and app established. Basic connection or app assistance may be available, but extensive network troubleshooting and complete system design are not automatically included.

Do you install commercial CCTV or provide alarm monitoring?

No. This service focuses on homeowner-owned residential smart cameras—not commercial CCTV, large surveillance networks, business systems or monitored alarm contracts.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the camera brand and model if known, the number of cameras, wide and close photos of each location, the power type and a short explanation of what you want each camera to see.
